A **learning cycle** is a concept of how people learn from experience. A learning cycle will have a number of stages or phases, the last of which can be followed by the first.

Siklus belajar merupakan konsep bagaimana cara orang belajar dari pengalaman. Bisa memiliki angka  atau , terakhir bagaimana bisa mungkin mengikuti oleh yang pertama.

#### Levels of reflection

Korthagen and Vasalos also described an [onion model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onion_model "Onion model") of "levels of reflection" (from inner to outer: mission, identity, beliefs, competencies, behavior, environment) inspired by [Gregory Bateson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregory_Bateson "Gregory Bateson")'s hierarchy of [logical types](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_theory "Type theory").In 2010, they connected their model of reflective learning to the practice of [mindfulness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mindfulness "Mindfulness") and to [Otto Scharmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otto_Scharmer "Otto Scharmer")'s [Theory U](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theory_U "Theory U"), which, in contrast to a learning cycle, emphasizes reflecting on a desired future rather than on past experience.&#8202;
